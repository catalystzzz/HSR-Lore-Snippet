Who is it that tells the fables? The ancient past, the vibrant present, and the fleeting future moments. The Fabulist walks upon the earth, passing through fields of flowers, and butterflies flutter toward him. He strolls along the shore, and fish splash their tails in agreement. In an age before the imagenae were even born, sprites of faint wisdom hid behind stones, secretly following, while birds carried away the seeds of stories, spreading them to countless homes.
The Fabulist walks farther and farther, his audience growing ever larger behind him. He set out from the ancient tree, journeying forward, only to return from the other end of the world. And so, all the living beings of the world gather around the storyteller, listening intently to myths and legends from the stars.
