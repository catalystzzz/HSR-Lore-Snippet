The segmentum where Planet Mio resides is pure chaos. A comet called "the Intruder" is always zooming around all over the place, constantly overlapping with Mio's orbit and threatening to crash into it. One time, as it approached Mio's atmosphere, someone on the planet cracked a joke that made the comet chuckle, causing it to lose its orbital trajectory. Just like that, an apocalyptic crisis passed Myo by. People realized: This star had a pretty low bar for humor.
Nowadays, whenever people on Mio spot "the Intruder" coming, they all start telling jokes to make it laugh and veer off course. Mio scientists are even trying to develop joke-powered propulsion to steer the comet in designated directions.

Vicino al Pianeta Mio, una cometa chiamata [[The Intruder]] gira sempre attorno alla sua atmosfera, e continua a sovrapporsi sulla sua orbita, minacciando di schiantarcisi.
Una volta, mentre si avvicinava, qualcuno nel pianeta disse uno scherzo che la ha fatta ridere e mandare fuori traiettoria.
Da quel momento, appena il popolo sentiva la cometa avvicinarsi, iniziavano a raccontare e dire scherzi per farla ridere.
