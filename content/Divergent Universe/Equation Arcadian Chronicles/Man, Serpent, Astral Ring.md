Give me your weary  
Your sorrowful  
Your dejected  
Those who lift their heads yearning for unbridled smiles  
Those abandoned by this [land of Elation](https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/Planarcadia "Planarcadia"), bereft of fun, faces heavy with gloom  
Give them to me  
Let one rest their hands upon my shoulders  
Then let another rest their hands upon theirs  
We touch one another, letting [Elation](https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/Elation "Elation") circulate through every organ in our bodies  
We form a serpent, spreading laughter and joy through every street and alley of the arcadia  
Every human we meet along the way, every [imagenae](https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/Imagenae "Imagenae"), will join our procession  
We are like a ravenous python, never tiring, never sated  
Until at last we connect head to tail around that [Phantasmoon](https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/Phantasmoon "Phantasmoon"), becoming its crystalline astral ring
