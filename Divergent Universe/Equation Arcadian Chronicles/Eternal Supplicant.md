[[Destruction]] e [[harmony]]

You have finally arrived. Have you come to call me to account for my crimes? I was the first to abandon the Phantasmoon Games and keep this mask for myself. As long as the game does not end, the Benediction of Elation shall never cease.
We conquered. We divided the land. We broke the mountains and split the rivers to make Dovebrook. We donned the masks of Fools and borrowed the great power of Aha, yet we styled ourselves as the lords. But what of it? This primitive world gained civilization through us. Those unknown monsters were burned to ash at our whim.
Maintainer of the game, come now. Strike off the head of this rule breaker. But before you do, tell me this: now that all of Ahatopia has fallen into my hands, this world ablaze with light... Is it not stunningly beautiful?

Un Supplicant decide di tenersi la maschera e bloccare la fine dei [[phantasmoon games]] così da mantenere la benedizione dell'[[ELATION]] in eterno.

"Hanno conquistato, diviso la terra, abbattuto montagne e diviso i fiumi per creare [[dovebrook]], ed indossato le maschere dei [[Masked Fool]] per prendere in prestito il potere di [[aha]], ed infine si sono auto proclamati "Lord".

Secondo lui però, questo fu il momento più bello per Ahatopia, il periodo in cui risplende di più, ed è quello che dice ad [[evanescia]] prima di farsi tagliare la testa.
