[[Propagation]]

Desire... The desire to break free from your shell. Countless wishes urge your birth. Abundant Wishpower envelops your body, while the whirring of mechanical arms rises and falls in waves. You feel a surge of discomfort. Your formless, chaotic consciousness ceases its struggle. Like liquid poured into a vessel, your outline becomes anchored, your self gains shape, and savagery is washed away by humanity.
You open your eyes. In an instant, instinct grants you memories, survival skills and impulses. There is also a yearning to achieve, a hunger to pursue. Live on, as all living things do. Then spend your entire existence filling the void deep within your heart.

Hypothesis: Studying the birth and behavioral patterns of imagenae in relation to Wishpower may assist current research on the Phantasmoon Games.
